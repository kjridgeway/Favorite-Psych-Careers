Psychology

MY EXPERIENCE

A Career Centered on Helping Others

Before I started working with HTML and CSS, I worked in the psychology field. I worked in a variety of different types of workplaces in psychology, but found that the same problems kept appearing: burnout, little pay, and increased mental health concerns for myself.

Regardless, I enjoyed this field a lot. I enjoyed working with people of all types, being able to help people, and overall felt rewarded by the work I was able to perform.

MY PROFESSIONAL BACKGROUND

Types of Psychology Jobs

Each role gave me a different opportunity to support people and make a positive impact on their lives.

01

ADVOCACY & COMMUNITY SUPPORT

Case Management

By far, this was the position I enjoyed the most. I assisted clients with multiple aspects of their lives, helping them access resources, work toward their goals, and navigate difficult situations.

My responsibilities included:

  • Assisting clients with applying for or renewing benefits.
  • Helping clients complete tasks they might not be able to manage independently, such as grocery shopping.
  • Advocating with multiple providers for clients' needs while encouraging self-advocacy.
  • Helping clients navigate challenging situations in their everyday lives.

The challenges

The difficult aspects of this position had a significant impact. I supported individuals experiencing frequent crises and worked 10-hour days, sometimes longer, while managing crises and determining the best course of action.

02

EDUCATION & INDIVIDUALIZED SUPPORT

Paraprofessional

This position involved working with children with autism. I supported students in a one-to-one setting and tracked their progress toward classroom goals.

Some students made significant progress, while others needed additional support and different approaches. Learning how to adapt to each student's individual needs was one of the most challenging parts of the job.

03

INDEPENDENT LIVING & DAILY CARE

Direct Support Professional

Direct Support Professionals help adults with everyday living activities and accessing their communities. Some individuals have physical or mental health concerns that make independent living more difficult.

My responsibilities included:

  • Assisting with cooking, cleaning, and daily living tasks.
  • Administering medications as part of my assigned duties.
  • Providing transportation to appointments and shopping.
  • Supporting participation in activities clients enjoyed.

What I Took Away From This Experience

These roles helped me develop empathy, communication, problem-solving, adaptability, and the ability to remain focused during challenging situations. Those skills continue to influence how I approach new opportunities and learn new things.
